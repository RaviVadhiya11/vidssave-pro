Connect your Instagram account

Official Instagram Login for your own Business or Creator account. After consent, this page can show media returned by Meta for that connected account.

Backend setup is required before connecting. See the setup guide in the GitHub repository.
Important: This integration lists media from the account that authorizes the app. It is not a downloader for arbitrary public Reels. Only save content you own or have permission to download. Meta may omit media URLs or make them temporary.
